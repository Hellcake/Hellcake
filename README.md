<p align="left">
  Hi! I'm Alexander Solovov, Head of Platform & Infrastructure at Algous Studio. I lead the infrastructure function while remaining hands-on with architecture, operations, automation, internal services, and incident response.<br><br>

  I design and operate Linux-based on-prem infrastructure for a VFX production environment spanning 57+ hosts, around 1 PB of raw storage, 10/40 GbE networking, centralized IAM, virtualization, monitoring, and production services.<br><br>

  <b>What I work on:</b><br><br>

  • <b>Linux platforms:</b> Debian, Ubuntu, Proxmox VE, ZFS, NFS, RAID, and Docker<br>
  • <b>Automation and delivery:</b> Ansible, PXE provisioning, Python tooling, and GitLab CI/CD<br>
  • <b>Networking and IAM:</b> MikroTik, nftables, OpenLDAP, and policy-based routing<br>
  • <b>Observability and reliability:</b> Zabbix, Grafana, Prometheus, backups, and incident response<br>
  • <b>Internal services and applied AI:</b> FastAPI, PostgreSQL, n8n, Ollama, and local LLM agents<br><br>

  Featured project: <a href="https://github.com/Hellcake/ai-helpdesk-automation"><b>AI Helpdesk Automation</b></a> — a production-deployed local LLM agent with safe tool calling, infrastructure integrations, and reproducible evaluation.<br><br>

  I hold a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University. My thesis focused on automating IT support in a VFX production studio and was deployed in the studio's production environment.
</p>
